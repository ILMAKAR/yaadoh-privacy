Yaadoh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Yaadoh is an Android app that reminds you before the dates on your documents, such as insurance, passports, warranties and subscriptions. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Yaadoh does not collect your data. It stays on your phone.

What Yaadoh does not do

  • It has no account, sign-up or login.
  • It has no server. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data off your phone.
  • It has no ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • It does not sell or share your information with anyone.

Photos of your documents

When you scan a document, the photo is read on your device to find the date and suggest a title and category. It is then discarded. Yaadoh does not keep the photo or the document, and nothing is uploaded. Reading uses on-device text recognition and, on supported phones, Google's on-device Gemini Nano model through ML Kit. Both run on your phone.

What is saved on your phone

For each reminder, Yaadoh saves only what the reminder needs: the title, the date, the notification schedule, and the details you choose to add, such as category, amount, currency, company and notes. If you enter your name, it is saved so the app can greet you. This information lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it.

Backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android Auto Backup may save the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone or after a reinstall. This is controlled by Android and your Google settings, not by Yaadoh, and it is covered by Google's privacy policy. Yaadoh has no access to these backups.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph a document you want to scan. You can also pick an image or add a reminder by hand.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Exact alarms: so reminders arrive on the day you chose.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule your reminders after the phone restarts.

Yaadoh Premium

The free version holds up to 10 active reminders. Yaadoh Premium is an optional yearly subscription bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Yaadoh does not see or store your payment details, and only learns whether your subscription is active. Google's handling of payments is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Yaadoh is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
